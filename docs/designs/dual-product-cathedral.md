## Vision

### 10x Check

LingMirror 不只是两个更大的软件。LingMirror Intelligence 成为可信决策证据层，Portfolio Launch OS 成为真实商业执行与结果层。用户从一个资金问题出发，看到证据、反证、权利和不确定性；批准后运行有损失上限的真实实验；最终系统能说明当初哪条判断正确、哪条错误、资金赚或亏在哪里。

两个产品保持独立代码库、数据库、身份、基础设施、审计域和 P&L，只通过客户授权、可撤回、版本化的商业契约交互。完整目标不是生成更多商品推荐，而是建立从证据、资金决策、真实成交到可校准学习的闭环。

### Platonic Ideal

客户打开系统时不需要理解 Agent、数据管道或模型。第一屏回答四件事：现在要决定什么、哪些事实可信、最大现金风险是多少、下一步为什么值得做。每一次批准都形成可回放的 decision receipt；每一次投入都受预注册门槛约束；每一次失败都进入有权利边界的负面知识；每一次结果都能在客户授权下反馈给原始判断。

系统的信任来自可执行责任，而不是免责声明：Intelligence 对证据来源、权利、版本与纠错提供有限 Evidence Warranty；Launch OS 对资金风险、审批、状态和最终贡献提供完整审计。产品不保证赚钱，但保证不会把估算伪装成事实、不会静默扩大风险、不会隐藏失败。

## Accepted Scope

### 1. Outcome Proof Protocol

- 内部 `dossier_id` 与 `launch_experiment_id` 永不离开各自产品；跨产品只使用为单一 consent/dossier/experiment 随机生成、不可推测且不可复用的 `pairwise_link_token`。
- 结果证明最少包含 outcome taxonomy、观察窗口、finalization 状态、关键偏差、撤回状态和 evidence warranty 影响。
- 客户必须逐用途授权；支持拒绝、撤回、到期和 crypto-erasure。撤回后只保留不可逆审计摘要。
- Intelligence 不得读取客户订单、供应商或商品明细；只接收合同允许的证明字段。

### 2. Evidence Warranty

- Warranty 只覆盖来源引用错误、权利判定失效、分类/版本错误、未满足冻结 critical claims、未按约定纠错或通知。
- 补救仅限免费更正、替代研究、服务额度或合同约定退款，设定责任上限。
- 明确排除销量、利润、税务、法律、海关裁定、平台政策变化和客户执行质量。
- Warranty claim 必须有状态、证据、Reviewer、响应时限、裁决、补救和审计记录。

### 3. Launch Capital Governor

- 每个实验预注册最大现金风险、不可取消承诺、库存上限、capital-days、观察窗口和 tranche 解锁条件。
- 缺少成本、资金来源、责任人或停止条件时不得进入生产投入。
- 系统可以阻断新的建议或要求重新审批，但不能自动支付、下单、增库存或加广告预算。
- 退款、拒付、物流补收和平台账单关闭前，贡献保持 provisional；只有 finalized 结果允许解锁 scale tranche。

### 4. Do-Not-Launch Library

- 统一记录 reject、abstain、failed、inconclusive 的原因、证据边界、适用市场/分类版本和重新评估条件。
- 默认仅客户私有；跨客户模式必须有明确授权、最小聚合阈值、重识别风险审查和撤回机制。
- 相似性只生成风险提示，不自动拒绝；关键决策必须回到当前证据与 Owner 审批。
- 禁止输出可识别客户、SKU、供应商、价格合同或原始经营事件的字段。

### 5. Outcome-Aligned Pricing Experiment

- 先收覆盖研究、数据、Reviewer 与服务成本的固定基础费。
- 结果费必须封顶，并只依赖双方预注册、可审计、已 finalized 的指标。
- `reject/abstain` 仍按基础费收费，避免系统偏向继续投入。
- 建议、评分和 Agent 上下文不得读取客户合同费率或潜在结果费。
- 只在少量设计伙伴合同中实验；未验证前不进入公共价目表。

## Outcome Proof Contract v1

### Trust Boundary and Identity

- The link is a random pairwise token minted for one customer consent, one dossier and one experiment. It is not a user ID, tenant ID or reusable global identity.
- Each product authenticates with an independent service principal and rotating key. Payloads are signed, timestamped and bound to audience, consent ID and nonce.
- Tokens cannot be reverse-resolved, reused across customers or joined to another export. Raw internal IDs remain inside the originating product.

### Minimum Envelope

```text
protocol_version, proof_id, pairwise_link_token
issuer_product, audience_product, consent_id, consent_version
purpose, issued_at, expires_at, nonce, signature_key_id
dossier_revision, experiment_revision
launch_state, sales_state, return_state, contribution_class
financial_state, observation_window
evidence_variances[], failure_reasons[], warranty_impact
payload_retention_until, consent_status, supersedes_proof_id
```

结果事实使用正交字段，不压成互斥单值：

- `launch_state = not_started | rejected | live | ended | inconclusive`
- `sales_state = no_sale | sold | partially_fulfilled | inconclusive`
- `return_state = none_observed | partial | full | window_open | inconclusive`
- `contribution_class = not_available | negative | zero | positive | inconclusive`

因此一次实验可以同时表达 `sold + partial return + positive contribution`，实现者不得发明枚举优先级。
`financial_state = provisional | closing | finalized | reopened | reversed`.

### Protocol Invariants

- Canonical serialization is deterministic UTF-8 JSON with sorted keys, fixed decimal/time formats and an explicit maximum envelope size. Oversize arrays are referenced by separately signed artifacts.
- Issuer time may differ from receiver by at most the configured clock-skew window; outside it, quarantine as untrusted.
- Signing keys are short-lived, rotated with overlapping verification windows and published by independent issuer key sets. Compromise immediately revokes the key, quarantines affected proofs and triggers reissue/review.
- Consumer support spans current major plus one announced compatibility window. Unknown major rejects atomically; no partial field import.
- Revocation is retried until signed consumer ACK or incident SLA expiry; missing ACK raises an alert and blocks all dependent use.
- Payload, retry and ACK limits are contract configuration, covered by conformance tests and cannot be relaxed per customer without versioning.

### Consent and Delivery Lifecycles

```text
Consent:  DRAFT -> ACTIVE -> EXPIRED
                    \-> REVOKED (terminal for new use; customer-triggered, unilateral)

Delivery: PREPARED -> SENT -> ACKNOWLEDGED -> SUPERSEDED
                    \-> DELIVERY_FAILED -> SENT (retry, not a business state)
          PREPARED/SENT/ACKNOWLEDGED -> VOID (issuer error)

Financial: PROVISIONAL -> CLOSING -> FINALIZED -> REOPENED -> FINALIZED
                                                \-> REVERSED
```

Only ACTIVE consent may issue. Duplicate nonce/proof_id is idempotently acknowledged. Consent REVOKED/EXPIRED immediately blocks new use and starts derivative invalidation. `retry` is an operation, never a persisted lifecycle state.

### Failure Matrix

| Failure | Named result | Producer action | Consumer action | Customer sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missing/expired consent | `consent_blocked` | do not issue | reject payload | proof not shared; renewal required |

| Timeout/5xx | `delivery_unavailable` | exponential retry with idempotency key | retain prior revision as stale | delayed, never silently current |

| Dispute | `proof_disputed` | freeze result fee | mark contested | named owner and deadline |

## Glossary and Metric Definitions

- `critical_claim`: order-frozen claim whose absence or insufficiency prevents `continue`.
- `positive account gross margin`: recognized product revenue minus data/API, LLM, infrastructure, payment, variable support, warranty and attributable integration cost, greater than zero for the defined account window.
- `natural repeat purchase`: a new paid decision initiated by the customer in its next ordinary decision cycle, not a free correction or bundled entitlement.
- `cohort`: all experiments whose decision was frozen in the same pre-registered market/platform/category/time window; rejects and failures remain in the denominator.
- `cross-cohort repeatability`: at least the pre-registered minimum number of independent cohorts meets contribution and risk thresholds without moving definitions after results.
- `capital-day`: one currency unit committed for one day until conservative recovery/final loss.
- `critical variance`: observed outcome outside the dossier's frozen assumption range and large enough to change the recommendation mapping.
- `minimum aggregation threshold`: versioned promotion configuration containing contributor floor, quasi-identifiers, attack model, test result, Reviewer artifact and expiry; no universal number is assumed and an approval boolean alone is invalid.
- `independent external paid orders`: orders from non-related external buyers or separately budgeted procurement decisions; excludes internal transfers, free corrections, bundled entitlements and duplicate invoices for one decision.

## Finalization and External Drift

- Each platform configures return, chargeback, fee adjustment and logistics surcharge windows.
- A conservative watermark ends the normal close window; late events inside a grace period reopen the result. Events after grace create a reversal adjustment and re-evaluate dependent tranche/fee decisions.
- `finalized` never means immutable forever; it means closed under the recorded policy and revision. UI and API always show policy, watermark and revision.
- Capital Governor begins in advisory mode. Enforcement is allowed only when every scoped spend入口 is integrated; otherwise it displays `external_spend_unenforced` and cannot claim a guaranteed ceiling.
- Before launch, the experiment must enumerate the full spend universe: product/sample, supplier commitments, inbound/outbound logistics, duties/tax, platform/payment fees, ads/promotions, returns/warranty, storage/liquidation, labor and known manual/off-platform channels. Any unknown or newly discovered manual spend invalidates the ceiling and automatically freezes the next tranche pending reconciliation.
- External drift, partial platform failure and manual spend trigger freeze/reconciliation, not silent continuation.
- Outcome pricing remains shadow billing until baseline, attribution window, adjustments, cap and dispute SLA reconcile on at least three finalized experiments. Finalized contribution is accounting evidence, not causal incrementality by itself.

## Warranty and Billing Dispute Lifecycles

```text
Warranty: OPEN -> TRIAGED -> ACCEPTED -> REMEDY_PENDING -> REMEDIED -> CLOSED
                     \-> REJECTED -> APPEALED -> ACCEPTED | FINAL_REJECTED
                     \-> ESCALATED (SLA breach/high liability -> Legal)

Settlement: SHADOW -> PROPOSED -> CUSTOMER_ACCEPTED -> INVOICED -> PAID
                         \-> DISPUTED -> RECONCILED -> PROPOSED | CANCELED
             FINALIZED outcome reopened -> FROZEN -> ADJUSTED | REVERSED
```

Only the named Warranty Reviewer may accept within cap; Legal decides above cap. One customer appeal is allowed by default. SLA breach escalates and pauses expiry; it never auto-rejects. Disputed or reopened outcome fees cannot invoice or collect until reconciliation. Finance Reviewer owns final settlement; all formulas, evidence and adjustments remain customer-visible.

Every P0 alert has a named on-call owner, customer notification template and incident review. Audit queries must reconstruct actor, consent, inputs, approval, revision, external reference, result and remedy without reading application logs alone.

## Downstream Impact Policy

When a source proof, consent or derived negative pattern is withdrawn, every dependent dossier is immediately marked `stale/needs_review` and blocked from new use. Customers are notified within the contract SLA with the affected claim and reason. The system attempts deterministic recomputation from remaining allowed evidence; if recomputation fails, recommendation becomes `abstain` and a Warranty Reviewer decides correction, replacement, credit or capped remedy. Past customer actions are never rewritten; their audit record links to the later invalidation and states what was known at decision time.

## Core State Machines

```text
Approval:  DRAFT -> PENDING -> APPROVED -> EXECUTING -> COMPLETED
                         \-> REJECTED      \-> FAILED/UNKNOWN
              material input change -> STALE -> new approval

Work Item: READY -> CLAIMED -> RUNNING -> WAITING -> RUNNING -> SUCCEEDED
                      \-> lease expired -> READY      \-> FAILED/BLOCKED/CANCELED

Operation: INTENT_RECORDED -> SENT -> COMPLETED
                                  \-> OUTCOME_UNKNOWN -> RECONCILING
                                                        -> COMPLETED | SAFE_RETRY
                                                        | PARTIAL | BLOCKED

Financial: PROVISIONAL -> CLOSING -> FINALIZED -> REOPENED -> FINALIZED
                                                \-> REVERSED
```

Impossible transitions are rejected by state/version checks and audited. Retries reuse the same idempotency identity; new material input creates a new revision rather than mutating history.
